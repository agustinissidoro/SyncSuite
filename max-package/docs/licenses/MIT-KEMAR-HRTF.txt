syncSuite.virtualspeakers~ and mc.syncSuite.virtualspeakers~ include HRIR data from the MIT KEMAR measurements
(diffuse-field equalized set, https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR/).

"This data is Copyright 1994 by the MIT Media Laboratory. It is provided free
with no restrictions on use, provided the authors are cited when the data is
used in any research or commercial application."

B. Gardner and K. Martin, "HRTF Measurements of a KEMAR Dummy-Head
Microphone", MIT Media Lab Perceptual Computing Technical Report #280, 1994.
